# A3 Finance V24 — Live Booking Management & Market Starter Rates

## Booking Management
- Live Limousine and Sakura records from Supabase storage.
- Open each row for full customer, schedule, journey/reservation, status, payment, amount and internal notes.
- Update, assign driver/table, complete, cancel or delete.
- New-booking notification count refreshes every 30 seconds.
- Limousine public API enforces a minimum six-hour advance booking rule.

## Rate Management
Use **Rate Management → Vehicle Rate → Load starter rates** to replace existing saved public rates with the Singapore market starter matrix.
Use **Rate Management → Additional Charges → Load starter charges** for the complete surcharge list.
Review all rates before publishing; competitor pricing changes and special-event pricing may differ.
