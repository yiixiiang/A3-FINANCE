# A3 Finance v5.2

- Cloud pulls are deferred while any record editor is open, preventing active forms from being replaced during synchronization.
- Food item Owner Company is selectable only by system administrators; company users see their automatically assigned owner as read-only.
- Added **Save & continue** for Food catalogue items.
- Added **Open 24 hours** for Food stalls.
- Added temporary closure messages.
- Added confirmation before discarding active edits and browser-leave protection.
- Improved mobile editors with full-screen forms, single-column fields, sticky actions and larger touch controls.
