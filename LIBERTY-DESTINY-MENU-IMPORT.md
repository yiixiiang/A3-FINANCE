# Liberty Destiny Food Menu Import

This release adds the supplied menu lists to Website Catalogue → Food under LIBERTY DESTINY.

## Stalls
- KL TRADITIONAL CHILLI BAN MEE
- KL CHICKEN RICE

## Imported catalogue
- 12 KL Traditional Chilli Ban Mee items
- 20 KL Chicken Rice food items
- 3 additional-charge items
- English and Chinese names
- SGD prices
- Best-seller notes where indicated

The Company column and Owner Company selector are removed from the Food catalogue interface. Company ownership remains stored internally for access control and cloud filtering.

The import runs once per browser profile and merges by website key, so existing matching items are preserved rather than duplicated.
